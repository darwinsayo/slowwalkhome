Most of life happens on the walk between things — between the car and the front door, between one season and the next, between who we were and who we're slowly becoming. This blog lives on that stretch of road.

Emmett Walker is a husband, a father, and a man who has learned (slowly, like most things) that the best thinking happens at a walking pace. He writes about faith and doubt, work and rest, the noise of a full house and the quiet after everyone's finally asleep — and whatever else turns up along the way.

There's no hurry here. Pull up a chair. Or better yet, walk a while.

---

*A word about the name:* Emmett Walker is a nom de plume. The man behind it would rather you meet the words than the writer. Besides, on a slow walk home, it's the road that matters — not who's walking it.
