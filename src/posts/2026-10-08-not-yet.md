I have always had more ideas than I knew what to do with.

That’s not bragging. It’s kind of the problem. Ideas are free. You can have a dozen before breakfast. But having the idea and being able to do something with it are two very different things, and for a long time I was stuck on the wrong side of that.

I knew what I wanted to build. I could picture it. I just didn’t have the skill, and I didn’t have the money to pay someone who did. So the ideas sat there. Some of them sat there for years.

I did try. More than once. I would get something up and running and then spend all my time keeping it from falling apart. Cleaning up messes I didn’t make. Fixing things that kept getting broken by people I’d never meet. I was busier maintaining than building. Not really building at all, if I’m being honest. It wasn’t fun, and I started to wonder if I just wasn’t cut out for it.

I wasn’t mad. Just tired.

Then something changed. I’m not going to say exactly what, partly because I’m not sure I understand all of it. But I found a way to do it that doesn’t cost me anything and doesn’t need me watching it all the time. It just works. And I could build again.

I still can’t quite believe it. Things I thought I needed a lot of money for, I didn’t. I just didn’t know where to look.

I keep thinking about all the ideas I let go because I figured I couldn’t. I wonder how many of them were fine and I just wasn’t ready yet. “Not yet” and “not ever” feel the same when you’re in the middle of it. They aren’t the same.

I think I felt like God wanted me to keep going, even when I didn’t see how. I could be wrong about that. But I kept going.

So now I have a lot of ideas to catch up on.
